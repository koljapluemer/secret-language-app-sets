I don't understand = ما فهمت
/ma fɪˈhɪmt/

You can soften it with آسف ما فهمت (aasif ma fhimt) = “Sorry, I didn’t understand.” (make this another vocab with `contains` including the main phrase)



---

To ask for clarification: شو يعني؟ (shū ya3ni?) = “What does that mean?”

شو (shū) → “what” Common in Levantine instead of Standard Arabic ماذا (mādhā).
يعني (ya3nī) → verb “it means”
